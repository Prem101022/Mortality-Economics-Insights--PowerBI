# Mortality-Economics-Insights--PowerBI
This project is a data visualization and analysis solution built in Power BI, focusing on evaluating child mortality rates across countries and understanding how economic conditions and health systems influence these outcomes.The goal is to gain insights into the factors affecting child mortality and health system performance using a comprehensive, multi-country dataset.
🧠 Project Objective:
To analyze and visualize the relationships between child mortality, economic indicators, and health system strength across various countries. The project explores patterns based on:
1.Child Mortality Rates (under-five mortality per 1,000 live births)

2.Economic Indicators (GDP, income levels, exports, imports)

3.Health System Metrics (per-capita health expenditure, life expectancy, total fertility rate)

📊 Tools Used

Power BI – For building interactive dashboards and advanced visualizations

📁 Dataset Source

Multi-country dataset including child mortality, economic, and health system data

🔍 Key Insights

Countries with higher per-capita health expenditure and stronger health systems have lower child mortality rates and higher life expectancy.

Economic factors such as GDP, exports, and imports are closely linked to health outcomes-wealthier countries generally perform better.

Visualizations like map charts, waterfall charts, and decomposition trees reveal clear disparities and highlight areas for improvement.

The interconnectedness of economic and health factors means that investing in healthcare and economic development together leads to better child health outcomes.

📌 Features of the Dashboard

Interactive Map Chart: Visualizes average GDP, imports, exports, and life expectancy by country

Slicers: Enable dynamic filtering by economic and health indicators

Waterfall & Area Charts: Track trends in child mortality, fertility, and life expectancy

Decomposition Tree Chart: Breaks down how factors like fertility rates, life expectancy, and health expenditure contribute to child mortality

Comparative Analysis: Easily compare countries’ strengths and weaknesses in healthcare and economic conditions

📝 Conclusion

The dashboard highlights the strong relationship between economic conditions, health system strength, and child survival.

Countries with robust health systems and higher economic resources consistently achieve better outcomes for children.

The analysis provides actionable insights for policymakers and stakeholders aiming to reduce child mortality and improve global health equity.
